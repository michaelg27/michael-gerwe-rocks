M — MIX: Syne + Righteous + Fredoka
Michael Gerwe
↑ Syne (wide)
Performer · Designer · Human

Hi, I'm Michael! I do what I love, whether it's acting, or whatever else. This is the gateway point to everything me.

How they mesh: Syne carries the big name/headlines — its quirky geometric weight gives the page a confident anchor. Righteous takes the role/tagline and section labels — its retro-theatrical flavor adds personality to short accent lines without competing with the headline. Fredoka handles body copy — rounded and friendly, it keeps long passages warm and easy to read so the page doesn't feel like three fonts fighting. All three share rounded/geometric bones, so despite having very different personalities, they sit together as "loud heading → playful accent → soft body" rather than clashing.
