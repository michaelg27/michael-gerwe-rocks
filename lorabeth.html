Lorabeth's Birthday

Lorabeth, lorabeth, lorabeth. Oh where to start. I am so grateful to have you in my life as a ride or die, a person who I can yap about anything to, a person who'll always listen no matter how heinous my next sentence will be. Seeing you be so optimistic and productive every day despite being so busy all the time fills me with a awe every day. You are a neverending fountain of kindness, love, generosity, sugar, spice, and everything nice. You are a wonderful friend, but simple sentences cannot describe how grateful I am to have you in my life. Here's an acrostic to attempt to fit all the wonder you are into simple words.

Loyal like a friend who I've known for millenia, despite only knowing you for a year. You keep all my secrets and I keep at least some of yours. It's M.A.D to stop being friends with each other, to which you loyally stick to.

Open to all conversations and potential hangouts. From playing Blood on the Clocktower on my birthday, grabbing bubble tea and talking about our mothers, and everything else, talking with you makes me feel open to all the joys that a friendship with you can bring.

Revered as a leader, you are a beacon of compassion to those around you. On the pitch, or in the classroom, you are definitely one that I and many others look up to for strength in all manners of the word.

Accepting of all, you are the definition of what it means to not judge a book by its cover. Your ability to accept and appreciate the best in everyone's hearts is only a testament to how bright yours shines.

Brainiacs quake when you walk into the room, intelligence incarnate, affluent academic, sophisticated speaker, and thorough thinker. Your curiosity to understand the world and to keep learning every single day is a joy to witness and be amazed by.

Everything I could ask for in a friend. You are one of the most valuable people in my life, and I cannot describe how much I admire you and long to know you better still.

Timeless is what I want our relationship to be. Beyond nations, borders, and all other constructs (nautical, geographical, or social) be damned!

Here's to a wonderful senior year together! Clink, or whatever onomatopoeia two plastic tea cups from Chinatown make.
