Productions
Emma (Play) Isabella · 2026

For my Advanced Play Production class in Junior Year, we put on a production of Ava Pickett's brilliant modern adaptation of Emma. The play is wonderful, cheesy, endlessly funny. Getting to play esteemed Bridezilla Isabella Woodhouse a joy in so many ways. It's not every day when you as a 6'4 tall person get to wear 4-inch heels and a dress for an hour! Jokes aside, I owe everything to the castmates and our director who created such an incredible space to learn and perform that I will forever cherish.

School Musicals 2023-2027

I wanted to highlight here all the high school musicals I've been in so far. I began performing with middle school musicals, so having the privilege to continue is something I'm always grateful for.. I've loved every minute of rehearsing and performing with these casts. Everyone is so incredibly talented, participates out of love and passion! The collective enthusiasm and dedication in the rehearsal is something that continues to infect me every single year.

Film and TV
Sherlock and Daughter (TV Series) Gerald Avery · 2025

I had the fortune to act in the incredible TV series Sherlock and Daughter as a day player. I learned so much from everybody on set, including David Thewlis and Blu Hunt, who were all so welcoming and skilled in their craft. Go watch it on CW!
