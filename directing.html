Directing

Making something sick on stage

I didn't expect directing to be a thing that I would like. In fact, my intro to the whole idea was because I just wanted to put on my own play with a bunch of friends at the Etcetera Theatre. I fell in love with the space when I heard about it, and I was still hungry for acting stuff, so I got two friends to essentially put on this play with me. Ever since then, I've kept throwing myself into directing opportunities where they come, or making more for myself! Here's them all.

Theatre
CMU Drama Precollege Directing ProjectDirector · 2025

Over the Summer of 2025, I was lucky enough to participate in Carnegie Mellon's PreCollege Drama Intensive. I learned so much about my process as an actor in that camp, but that's not why I'm mentioning it here. During the camp, I took Carlos Martinez's godsend directing class. The class was so valuable, and taught me so much about what exactly it means to be a director. Working under his mentorship along with all the other students was a blessing beyond naming. For our final project, we wrote and directed a scene related to a specific scene from Caryl Churchill's Love and Information. We were split into partners, and I acted in one person's final project and they acted in mine (shoutout to Leo!). I'm really proud of what I accomplished, and the stunning theatre everyone in the group made. Thank you Carlos and everyone else.

Not Not Not Not Not Enough Oxygen by Caryl ChurchillDirector and Mick · 2025

I produced, directed, and acted in a production of Caryl Churchill's Not Not Not Not Not Enough Oxygen at the Etcetera Theatre in Camden. This was the play that got me into directing, which also meant that I had no clue what it meant to direct a play. I jumped in headfirst anyways! With no experience but plenty of ideas, I stumbled around with two friends until we found something that worked and was performable. We performed twice at the Etcetera Theatre in Camden. Whether it was good or not is a mystery, but we had fun. Nothing else really matters. When I read the play, I knew that I had to do something with it. Caryl Churchill's genius is beyond measure, and I wanted to challenge myself with such a crazy, dystopian, absurdist, yet deeply truthful script. The producing aspect of it was a nightmare in and of itself. It really gave me a firsthand perspective into why making theatre is so absurdly expensive. Despite all the challenges, it was more fun than I could have ever imagined.

Lunch (Short Film) Co-Director · 2026

I had the honor and joy of being a co-director for the short film Lunch. This was my first every film directing gig, and I'm so lucky to have had my other co-director who had way more experience than me in the industry. Learning from them was an utter joy, and a huge step into a medium that's outside of my comfort zone. We premiered along with several other short films at the Everyman Maida Vale in London, and seeing the 14 minutes I had spent the past year working on with my co-director right there on a giant screen for the first time was addicting to say the least.
